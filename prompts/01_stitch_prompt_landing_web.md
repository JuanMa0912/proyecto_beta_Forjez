# Prompt para Stitch — Landing page FORJEZ Consulting

Create a high-fidelity responsive landing page design for a consulting firm named FORJEZ Consulting.

Use this brand context:

- Brand: FORJEZ Consulting
- Tagline: “Donde la estrategia se convierte en resultados”
- Core promise: FORJEZ transforms a company's operational potential into sustainable and measurable results.
- Personality: strategic, executive, trustworthy, sober, premium, clear, business-oriented.
- Target users: business owners, general managers, operations directors, administrative leaders, and growing companies that need operational order, control, scalability, and better decision-making.

Visual direction:

- Clean corporate layout.
- Premium but not flashy.
- Deep petroleum blue, strategic green, sober gold accent, light neutral backgrounds.
- Strong typographic hierarchy.
- Spacious sections.
- Rounded cards.
- Subtle shadows.
- Professional dashboard/process visuals.
- Avoid generic startup gradients and excessive illustrations.

Required sections:

1. Sticky header with logo placeholder, navigation and CTA.
2. Hero section with a strong headline about turning operations into measurable results.
3. Subheadline explaining that FORJEZ diagnoses, designs and implements efficient, scalable operating systems.
4. Primary CTA: “Solicitar diagnóstico”.
5. Secondary CTA: “Ver servicios”.
6. “Problemas que resolvemos” section with 4 cards:
   - Crecimiento sin control operativo.
   - Procesos no documentados.
   - Baja visibilidad para tomar decisiones.
   - Riesgos y reprocesos internos.
7. Services section with 3 pillars:
   - Estructuración y Optimización de Operaciones.
   - Control Organizacional y Eficiencia.
   - Sistemas para la Toma de Decisiones.
8. Methodology section with 4 steps:
   - Diagnosticar.
   - Diseñar.
   - Implementar.
   - Medir y ajustar.
9. Impact section with placeholder metrics clearly marked as examples, not real numbers.
10. Contact CTA section.
11. Footer with phone, email and social placeholder.

UX requirements:

- Provide desktop and mobile versions.
- Include hover/focus states for buttons.
- Include form field states if a contact form is shown.
- Use Spanish copy in the interface.
- Make the design ready to be implemented in Next.js and Tailwind.
- Include reusable component ideas: Hero, ServicePillarCard, MethodStep, MetricCard, ContactCTA, Footer.

Important:
Do not invent real client logos, certifications, testimonials, or metrics. Use clear placeholders where information is missing.
