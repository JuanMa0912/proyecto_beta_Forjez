# FORJEZ Brand Strategy Skill

## Purpose

Generate credible, strategic and conversion-oriented content for FORJEZ Consulting.

## Required context

Before writing, use:

- `docs/brand_context.md`
- `DESIGN.md` if the content appears in UI

## Workflow

1. Identify the business objective.
2. Identify the target audience.
3. Choose the right tone: executive, strategic, clear, reliable.
4. Write copy that connects operational problems with measurable outcomes.
5. Remove exaggerated claims.
6. Mark placeholders for missing facts.

## Copy principles

- Lead with business pain.
- Explain operational value.
- Show method and control.
- Close with action.
- Prefer clarity over decoration.

## Forbidden

- Fake metrics.
- Fake client names.
- Fake testimonials.
- Legal guarantees.
- Claims like “líder absoluto” without proof.

## Output

Provide:

- Final copy.
- Short variant.
- CTA suggestions.
- Notes about assumptions.
